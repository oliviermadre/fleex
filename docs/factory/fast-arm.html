Fast arms

A fast arm swings twice as fast, there and back, as a fast belt carries twice as fast. It keeps its own colour (the one you gave it, or amber when it sorts), so "fast" is said apart from the colour, in the fast belts' blue. Below, a plain arm on the left and a fast one on the right, in each look.

1 · How a fast arm looks

A

A blue pivot

plainfast

Its base has a double ring in the fast blue, as a fast belt has blue rails. It reads at rest and from far.

B

plainfast

The arrow painted under it carries a pair of blue chevrons, those of a fast belt. The same sign as the belts, on the floor, under the arm.

recommended
plainfast

A, plus a short blue trail behind its cup while it swings. At rest, the pivot says it; at work, the trail does.
