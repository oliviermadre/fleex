Messages on the floor

On the Factory, notifications stop being cards in the middle of the screen. They become lines of text over the floor, with no plate behind them. Each one fades by itself, and a click on the floor goes through to the floor. This is still Fleex's one toast(): the Factory page asks the Toaster to draw its stack this way, so every message keeps its tone, its action (Undo) and its link. Errors stay longer, and a message that waits for something ("Build running…") stays until it is done.

1 · Where the messages are

A

Bottom left, as chat in an RPG

Above the minimap, the latest at the bottom, fading after a few seconds. Out of the way, but far from the list of what needs you, and the lines get long over the floor.

B

In the middle, over the console, with a log

recommended

In the notch over the saved selections, where nothing else is, as chat is in a strategy game. They fade by themselves. Enter (with nothing selected) opens the log there: the last messages, to scroll and to search.

C

Under what needs you

The latest at the top, pushing the others down as they fade. Everything about you is in one corner, but a long list of needs pushes them down the screen.

Enter: talk to the floor

Enter opens a line over the console, as chat does in a strategy game. Who you talk to is written at its start, and Tab changes it: [Assistant], or [#788 unipile], the ticket selected (or the first one that needs you). Replies come back as lines of the log, fading like the rest. Try it on the screen above: open the log, type, Tab, Enter.

To the assistant

A question about the floor or anything else: "why is #788 stuck?", "what failed today?". It is the Assistant of Fleex, with the floor in view; its answer comes back in the log, and opens whole in the Assistant page.

To a ticket

A comment in its thread, as you. When the ticket waits on a question, it is the answer: the station that asked runs again. An @persona in it starts that agent, as anywhere in Fleex. Its agents' replies come back in the log.

3 · Who you can talk to

recommended

The assistant and the tickets. Tab goes from one to the other; with a ticket selected, it goes through the assistant and that ticket.

B

Tickets only

A way to answer and comment without opening the ticket. The assistant stays on its page.

C

The assistant only

A console to ask about the floor. Answering a ticket still opens it.

4 · Who the line opens on

A

The ticket selected, else the last one talked to

recommended

Select a ticket, Enter, type: you answer it. Nothing selected: whoever you talked to last. Enter with a ticket selected then means "talk to it", and its thread opens with V or a double-click.

B

Always the assistant

Enter keeps opening a ticket when one is selected; with nothing selected, it opens the line on the assistant. Tab to reach a ticket.
