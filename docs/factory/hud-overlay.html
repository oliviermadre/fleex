The Factory without its top bar

The title bar reads fleex.factory on this page and fleex.dev elsewhere. That one is cheap: the desktop app draws its own title bar, so it only has to follow the page.

recommended

Symmetric, but over the middle of the floor, where the work is.

2 · Where the filter popup opens

Either way, the Filters tab on the minimap counts the filters on and the tickets shown, "/" opens it with the cursor in the search, and Escape closes it. The filters keep working with the popup closed.

A

Out of the minimap, with no backdrop

recommended

It rises from its tab, the floor stays live behind it, and what is filtered fades as you click.

B

A modal in the middle

More room, but it covers the floor you are filtering.

3 · Where the emergency stop goes

A

On the console's top edge, in the middle

recommended

Always there, in build mode too, and away from the buttons you click all day. Hiding the console (U) leaves it on the floor's bottom edge.

B

In a strip over the command card

As the menu sits over the action buttons in a game: Stop, Menu and Hide together. Close to the buttons you press without looking.

C

On the minimap's corner

Next to Filters, with the floor-wide tools. The left side gets busy.
