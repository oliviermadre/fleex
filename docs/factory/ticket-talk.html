Talking to a ticket

When a station sets a ticket aside because its agent asked you something, the ticket writes in the log: From #788 unipile · Spec, then its question. If the agent gave closed choices ("staging or prod"), they are buttons: a click answers. The line then opens on that ticket, and what you type goes into its thread as a comment from you. Since the ticket waits on a question, your comment is the answer, and the station runs again. A gate does not write: talking to it posts a comment, and the decision stays on its buttons.

The log, as it plays

Click a choice, or type an answer and press Enter. The gate line at the end shows what a gate says: nothing in the log by itself, and only a comment when you talk to it.

1 · Which set-asides write to you

A

A question, and a failed run

recommended

The agent's question, with its choices; or "Build failed: exit 1 in pnpm test", with Run again and Logs. A ticket you set aside yourself says nothing: you know why.

B

A question only

A failed run stays in the list of what needs you and the usual messages, without a line of its own in the log.

2 · Replying to a failed run

A

A comment; Run again stays a link

recommended

What you type goes into its thread. Running again is a click on its line, or the command card. No run starts because of a remark.

B

A comment, and it runs again

As for a question: your reply is the instruction for the next run ("the test is flaky, run it again"). Quicker, but every remark costs a run.

3 · Where the line opens

A

On the last ticket that wrote to you

recommended

Enter answers whoever just spoke, as a reply in a chat. A ticket selected comes first, then the last one that wrote, then the first that needs you.

B

As now

The ticket selected, else the first that needs you. Tab still reaches the others.

4 · A ticket already waiting when the page opens

A

Its question shows when you talk to it

recommended

Open the line on a ticket that waits on a question you have not seen here: its question is put in the log first, with its choices, so you never answer blind.

B

Only what happens while the page is open

A question asked before you arrived is in the ticket's thread, not in the log.
