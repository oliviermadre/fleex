A zone whose tickets need you

Today "Needs you" lists what waits on a station, a gate or the floor, never what stands in a zone. A zone such as Doing in "Manual stuff" holds tickets that are yours to do: ticked, its tickets are listed with the others, longest wait first, and N goes through them.

It names the place on screen: plain, but about the screen rather than the work.

C

"Mine to do"

Short, and says who does them.

2 · Which of its tickets

A

Every ticket in it

recommended

As long as a ticket stands in the zone, it is listed, released or not. Once an arm takes it out, it leaves the list.

B

Only those not yet released

A ticket you released, waiting for an arm, is no longer listed. In a zone that releases on its own, that lists nothing.

3 · What the line says it waits for

A

"Doing · waiting for you"

recommended

The zone's name, then what every line of the list says in its own words.

B

"Doing · in your hands"

It says the work is yours to do, not something to decide.

C

"Doing · doing"

The status the zone sets. Shortest, but it says the same thing twice when the zone is named after it.
